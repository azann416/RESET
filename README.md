# RESET

Your journey. Your pace. 💗  

Everyone deserves a fresh start, and no one should have to figure it all out alone, especially when it comes to addiction. 

RESET is a marketing campaign and a space for real conversations about addiction, recovery, and breaking the stigma. Explore interactive challenges, learn something new, collect digital rewards, and discover little ways to support yourself or someone you care about. As well as, a built-in tracker to track one's progress. 

Whether you're starting your own journey, supporting a friend, or just here to learn, you belong here.

No judgment. No pressure. Just progress, one step at a time.

- Based in Toronto, Canada.
